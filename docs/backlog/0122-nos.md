## Why
The user asked: "we need NOS". The Flux/88 mph warp is the signature mechanic, but it's a
one-shot. Racers want a short boost they control.

## What
- `src/nos.js` (pure and deterministic):
  - **Tank:** sized by the car's FLUX/BOOST rating, 1.9 s + 0.12 s per point (rating 5 = 2.5 s). Starts half full.
  - **Burn:** holding burns it for +26 mph/s, with the cap raised 18 mph over top speed and never past the warp ceiling, so the live-room and leaderboard speed bounds still hold.
  - **Refill:** drifting, airtime, near misses, 5 s of clean driving, and pickup canisters (`addNos(n, 'canister')` for the track's canisters).
- **Controls:** Shift (or N while racing), gamepad LB, and a NOS button in the touch action arc. It replaces HONK and keeps its id, so saved layouts still load.
- **Feel:** blue-violet exhaust flames, an FOV push-in, speed lines and a boost haptic. Calm mode skips the push-in and speed lines.
- **HUD:** a NOS gauge under FLUX that glows while burning.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] The tank burns 1 s per second, cuts out when empty, and doesn't flicker on when nearly empty
- [x] Each refill source works; nothing refills while burning; a penalty resets the clean timer
- [x] Speed never passes the warp ceiling; no car beats another at everything (incl. NOS)
- [x] Touch presets still have no overlaps with the NOS button in the arc
